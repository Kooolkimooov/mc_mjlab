# Joint residual with motion authority

## LocomanipMotionAction

**Current:** The `locomanip_motion` task adds one action after the existing
joint-position residual. Zero or negative values keep normal speed; `0.5`
requests half speed; values at least `0.95` request a hold. Intermediate
nonzero speeds are bounded below by 0.1. The bridge dispatches the resulting
scalar through `Locomanip::setMotionScale` only with fresh bilateral Hold
and healthy controller/worker status. Each reset row returns to normal speed.

The actor and critic observe the dispatched scale, controller-applied scale,
motion state and post-hold resume limit. Joint magnitude/rate penalties describe joint
offsets. Motion diagnostics are separate because speed scale is dimensionless.
The action and observation changes have distinct full task IDs and experiment
names, generated by the naming helper. The original joint task remains usable
with its existing action interface.

**Re-measure if:** the action mapping, phase gating, callback implementation,
controller tuning or payload changes.

**History:** Added on 2026-10-14 after the joint residual was found to have no
explicit command for reducing the controller's walking/cart motion demand.

## setMotionScale

**Current:** The callback lives in the external LocomanipController source,
alongside its `ManipManager` implementation, in controller commit `91570d6`.
`motionScale` reports the
controller's scale and `motionState` reports 0 for following the route, 1 for
braking and 2 for holding. Normal speed does not change the original trajectory.
Scale changes of at least 0.1 initiate a stop/replan transition during Hold.
The callback rejects nonfinite or out-of-range scales; near-zero requests mean
hold. It applies to waypoint motion, not the controller's separate velocity mode.

The transition clears future footsteps while retaining an active swing.
`MotionStopTrajectory` wraps a copy of the current object trajectory and
continuously reduces its time derivative to zero over at least 0.5 seconds,
waiting for the retained step to finish. The cart reference then remains fixed.
The FSM keeps both hands in Hold instead of proceeding to release.
When a positive scale is requested, the remaining waypoint goals are restored
with longer durations and footsteps are regenerated to follow them. Partial
segments use a duration bound that preserves peak velocity and acceleration
when the interpolation restarts from rest. A one-second
lead-in lets the controller prepare the next step. The final cart goal is retained.
After an explicit hold, resumed motion is capped at half speed until episode reset;
`motionScaleLimit` exposes that cap. This is a conservative restriction following
an observed loaded failure when resuming at full speed. Normal speed remains
available before the first hold.

A held episode still ends at the configured wall-clock cap. Holding does not
set `Locomanip::complete`, and an incomplete episode is not a safe completion.
A full training run must evaluate both failure and completion/displacement;
surviving by remaining stationary is not evidence of finishing the task.

**Re-measure if:** the footstep queue cancellation, object interpolation,
FSM completion guard or timing changes. The controller module and companion
library hashes bind new checkpoints and authority reports to this implementation.

**History:** The previous `clearWaypointQueue()` utility noted a discontinuous
object-velocity change. This control path retains the incoming reference velocity
and applies the chain rule while braking, rather than replacing a moving reference
with a zero-velocity interpolation in one step.

## MOTION_EPISODE_SCALE

**Current:** The new task allows `2 * 51.66 = 103.32` seconds per episode,
giving the post-hold half-speed route enough time to complete. The original
joint-residual task retains its cap. Completion still ends an episode promptly,
and a prolonged hold or very low speed can still exhaust the new cap.

**Re-measure if:** the nominal route, post-hold speed limit or measured
completion duration changes.

**History:** At 525 kg the measured half-speed route took 82.36 seconds and
the four-second hold followed by half-speed resume took 86.40 seconds. The old
51.66-second cap would classify both as incomplete.

## probe_locomanip_motion.py

**Current:** The probe runs first episodes at fixed mass and seed, with zero joint
residuals and encoder bias disabled. It compares normal motion, a half-speed
request and hold/resume after 0.15 m of commanded cart progress. It checks actual
controller readouts, stationary reference and physical robot during Hold,
preserved final goal, completion and physical safety. Traces and run provenance
are written before row resets. The probe extends its cap to accommodate the
requested slowdown and hold beyond the registered training cap.

```sh
uv run python scripts/probe_locomanip_motion.py --mass 10 \
  --registered-cap --output logs/locomanip/motion-check/mass-10
```

**Re-measure if:** the controller, observations, residual routing or simulator
changes. These probes establish command authority, not learned-policy improvement.
Use `--registered-cap` to keep the task's 103.32-second allowance; omit it when
testing a longer hold/reset schedule.

**History:** The native `motion_stop` CTest checks pose/velocity continuity,
zero terminal velocity, the time-warp derivative, invalid duration handling
and restart velocity/acceleration bounds for cubic and bang-bang interpolation.
Python contracts check neutral action, phase gating, actual input feeds and
partial-reset isolation.

## Motion probes on 2026-10-14

**Current:** HRP5P, seed 42, encoder bias off, one first episode per condition
and mass, with all joint residuals zero. Each intervention began after 0.15 m
of commanded cart progress, around 20 seconds. This probes command authority;
it does not test a learned trigger or estimate training-run success probability.
Results and terminal-safe traces are under
`logs/locomanip/motion-2026-10-14/mass-*-conservative*/`.

| mass | baseline | half speed | hold then conservative resume |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 kg | completed 49.66 s | completed 82.80 s | completed 122.82 s after a 40 s hold |
| 100 kg | completed 49.28 s | completed 82.42 s | completed 86.44 s after a 4 s hold |
| 525 kg | completed 49.24 s | completed 82.36 s | completed 86.40 s after a 4 s hold |
| 550 kg | fell at 42.10 s | completed 82.44 s | completed 86.42 s after a 4 s hold |

All eight intervention episodes completed without a combined or worker failure
under the extended probe cap. The 40-second hold exceeds the registered task's
time allowance when followed by the measured resume; it tests hold/reset
plumbing, not completion within the training cap. The cart reference remained
stationary throughout each hold. Robot forward drift during the 40-second
10 kg hold was 0.0080 m; during the four-second 525 kg hold it was 0.0200 m.
The 10 kg hold spanned another row's terminal reset without losing its state.
Every requested scale matched the actual native input column. Final goals
matched the baseline at the three masses where baseline completed.

The native companion library hash for these four probe groups was
`a37d6304b54c3d8c3e91caa901b8940e69477e7201d72515d00b86b96cfb94b6`;
their individual reports retain the remaining controller provenance. At 550 kg
the probe exits nonzero because its baseline-safety and all-complete checks fail;
the two intervention outcomes remain separately reported.

The final committed controller build was checked again at 525 kg under the
registered 103.32-second cap, one episode per arm. Baseline completed at
49.26 seconds, half speed at 82.42 seconds, and hold/resume at 86.38 seconds;
all three were safe. This report is under `mass-525-final-build/` and records
controller commit `91570d618902930d78671d607a58f912b427933a` and companion
library hash `a621349832ab9f9d2bb5c160f68cad1fcb86d2ff0992343296f88dc4c3820915`.

**Re-measure if:** the trigger, mass, bias setting, controller binary/config,
reset routing or simulation changes. Repeated trials and held-out conditions
are required before treating the 550 kg result as a policy qualification.

**History:** Two earlier 525 kg hold/resume trials restarted at full speed and
collapsed at 35.50 and 38.56 seconds, although Hold itself stayed upright.
The second already included restart interpolation bounds. This motivated the
post-hold half-speed limit; the final conservative trial completed at 86.40 s.
